About Me

The mind behind
the content.

“Design is not just what it looks like. Design is how it works.”

I'm Vishal Singh, a Digital Content Creator from Varanasi. I hold a BCA (2022–2025) and completed the 402-hour Digital Content Creation program at Arena Animation (2024–2026), where I trained in design, motion, video and web development.

I like to take an idea all the way: design the look in Figma, Photoshop or Illustrator, bring it to life with motion in After Effects, cut and sound-design it in Premiere Pro, and build it as a responsive website. I use AI tools to move faster and try more ideas — but every final piece is shaped by hand, with care for typography, colour and timing.
